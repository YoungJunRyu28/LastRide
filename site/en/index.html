How it works

Three steps. That’s it.

Set your way home once, then tap before the night out.

1

Set your way home

Pick your home station (or an address). No account needed.

2

Start tonight

LastRide finds your nearest station and last train and shows when to leave — updating as you move.

3

Get the reminder

A notification or alarm at the times you choose: 30 or 15 minutes before, or at leave time.

Features

Everything to make the last train.

From the reminder to leave to a plan B if you miss it.

When to leave

Works out when to leave from the walk to the nearest station and the last train. Choose how much time to spare.

Reminders & alarms

Notifications at the times you choose. On iOS 26 and later, alarms that ring even in silent mode.

Night tracking

Updates your leave time as you move, and stops by itself once the last train has gone.

If you miss it

First trains, taxi estimates, and nearby internet cafés and hotels to wait until morning.

Widgets & lock screenPremium

Tonight’s leave time on your home screen, and a lock-screen countdown in the last hour.

Groups for nights out

Share only your leave time with friends — never your location or home. Creating a group is Premium; joining is free.

FAQ

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Your destinations and settings stay on your iPhone, and no movement history is kept.

How is my location used?

Only to find the nearest station and walking time. It’s used while the app is closed only if you choose “When closed” for night tracking.

Is the app in English?

Yes. LastRide works in English and Japanese — switch any time in Settings.

Is there an Android version?

The iPhone version comes first.
